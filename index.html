FAQs

Is ad spend included in the Ads packages?

No. Our fee covers strategy, setup and management. Ad spend is paid directly to the platform (Google, Meta, LinkedIn) from your own account.

Is there a lock-in period for monthly plans?

Monthly retainers have a 3-month minimum so strategies have time to deliver results. After that, cancel anytime with 30 days notice.

Are prices inclusive of GST?

Prices shown exclude 18% GST, which is added on the final invoice. The quote basket shows an estimate including GST.

Can I customise a package?

Absolutely. Add any combination of services to your quote basket and send it to us — we'll tailor the plan to your budget.
