    FAQ

    Questions

    Is ad spend included in the ads packages?

    No. Our fee covers strategy, setup and management. Ad spend is paid directly to Google, Meta or LinkedIn from your own account.

    Do you shoot on location?

    Yes. We shoot in Agra and Delhi NCR. Travel outside these areas is quoted separately.

    Is there a lock-in period for monthly plans?

    Monthly plans have a 3-month minimum so the content and ads have time to work. After that, cancel with 30 days' notice.

    Are prices inclusive of GST?

    Prices exclude 18% GST, which is added on the invoice. The quote basket shows an estimate including GST.

    Can I customise a package?

    Yes. Add any services to your quote and send it on WhatsApp. We will tailor the plan to your budget.
