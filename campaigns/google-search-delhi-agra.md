# Google Ads Campaign Plan: YAP Digital Marketing

**Status:** Ready to build. Waiting on (1) a connected Google Ads account and (2) a public landing page URL.

## Campaign settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Campaign name | `YAP | Search | Leads | Delhi NCR + Agra` |
| Type | Search (Google Search only, Search Partners **off**, Display Network **off**) |
| Goal | Leads (WhatsApp clicks + form enquiries) |
| Daily budget | **₹100/day** (about ₹3,000/month) |
| Bidding (weeks 1–3) | Maximize Clicks, max CPC limit **₹35** |
| Bidding (after about 15 conversions) | Switch to Maximize Conversions |
| Locations | Delhi, Gurugram, Noida, Greater Noida, Ghaziabad, Faridabad, Agra |
| Location option | "Presence: people **in** or regularly in your targeted locations" (not "interest in") |
| Language | English + Hindi |
| Ad schedule | Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM – 9:00 PM |
| Devices | All (mobile-heavy; no bid adjustments at first) |
| Landing page | `https://<your-live-site>/#catalog` |

### What ₹100/day realistically buys
Search CPCs for "social media agency delhi"-type keywords usually run ₹25–60.
Expect roughly **2–4 clicks a day, 60–110 clicks a month**. At a 5–10% enquiry rate, that is about
**3–10 leads per month**. That is enough to learn which keywords work. Raise the budget on the ad group that brings leads.

## Conversion tracking (set up before launch)
1. Create a Google Ads conversion action "WhatsApp click" (website, *Contact* category).
2. Fire it when someone clicks a `wa.me` link (via Google Tag Manager, a click trigger on URLs containing `wa.me`).
3. Add a second conversion for phone-call clicks (`tel:` links).

## Weekly checklist (first 4 weeks)
- Search terms report: add irrelevant searches as negatives.
- Pause keywords with 40+ clicks and 0 leads.
- Move budget toward the ad group with the lower cost per lead.
- After about 15 conversions, switch bidding to Maximize Conversions.

## Note on budget split
For **cinematic content and reels**, Instagram/Facebook (Meta) ads usually work better than Google Search
because people need to *see* the work. Once Google Search is bringing leads, consider adding ₹100–150/day on
Meta with a 15-second showreel targeting business owners in Delhi NCR & Agra.
